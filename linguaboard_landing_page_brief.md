# LinguaBoard — Landing Page Brief
**Page:** linguaboard.com.ng  
**Type:** Prelaunch — Community & Waitlist  
**Status:** Design Complete → In Development  
**Version:** 1.0 — August 2025

---

## 1. What This Page Is

This is not a sales page. There is nothing to buy yet.

This is a **prelaunch community page** — its only job is to find the schools that feel the problem most acutely, tell them something real is being built to solve it, and invite them to be part of shaping it before it launches.

The page exists between the idea and the product. It is proof that LinguaBoard is real, intentional, and being built by someone who understands the Nigerian school context — not a foreign company guessing at it.

---

## 2. Single Goal

**Collect school registrations — name and email — from proprietors or principals who want to be part of the pilot or early launch cohort.**

Every design decision, every line of copy, every section of this page answers one question: does this move a school owner toward filling in the form?

## 3. Brand System

### Colours
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Teal | #0D7377 |
| Secondary | Turquoise | #14BDCC |
| Accent | Amber Gold | #F4A832 |
| Base | Warm Cream | #F5F0E8 |
| Text | Charcoal | #1C1C1C |
| Surface | White | #FFFFFF |

**Gold rule:** Amber Gold is used ONLY on the primary CTA button and nowhere else on the page. The moment it appears elsewhere it loses its function as an attention signal.

**Section rhythm:** Dark Teal → Cream → Dark Teal → Cream → Dark Teal. Never two sections of the same type consecutively.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Nunito Bold — large, lowercase style, confident
- **Body:** Inter Regular — 16px minimum, 1.7 line height
- **Labels:** Inter Medium — 11px, uppercase, tracked

### Design Language
- Asymmetric layouts (text left / visual right, alternating)
- Warm Nigerian-feeling illustrations — not stock photography
- Rounded pill buttons only
- Flat design — no gradients, no shadows, no glow
- Aggressive whitespace in cream sections

## 5. Copy Rules

- **Lowercase headlines** — signals confidence, not carelessness. Duolingo built this convention. LinguaBoard inherits it.
- **No jargon** — never say "pedagogy", "edtech", "AI-powered", "innovative", "world-class"
- **Naira over dollars** — all pricing references use ₦
- **Second person always** — "your school", "your class", "your board" — never "schools" in the abstract
- **Short sentences in body copy** — if a sentence runs past 20 words, break it
