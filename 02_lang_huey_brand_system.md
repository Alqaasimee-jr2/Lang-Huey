# Lang Huey — Brand System
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** August 2025
**Status:** Locked

---

## 1. Brand Story

**One sentence:**
Built by a Nigerian son, for his mother's school — and every school like hers.

**Long form:**
Lang Huey started because a school in Lagos had a French class and no French teacher. The founder's mother was spending money on virtual lessons that dropped every time NEPA struck. She had no control, no consistency, and no solution in sight.

Her son built one. Offline. Structured. One-time. On the smartboard already on her wall.

That story is the brand. It is specific, it is true, and it is the same story thousands of Nigerian school proprietors are living right now.

---

## 2. Brand Pillars

### Reliable
Works every time. No internet. No excuses. No dropped connections. No NEPA problems. Lang Huey runs the class whether or not the WiFi does.

### Structured
Government curriculum. One class. One language. Nothing extra, nothing missing. Every lesson maps to the NERDC scheme of work the school is already required to follow.

### In Control
The school owns it. The teacher runs it. No third party involved. No platform dependency. No vendor who can raise prices or shut down. Yours permanently.

### Accessible
One-time payment. Any staff member operates it. Built for Nigeria, priced for Nigeria, designed around Nigeria's infrastructure reality.

## 3. Colour System

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Teal | #0D7377 | Hero sections, nav, headers, app backgrounds |
| Secondary | Turquoise | #14BDCC | Icons, highlights, teacher cue bar, hover states |
| Accent | Amber Gold | #F4A832 | CTA buttons and key numbers ONLY |
| Base | Warm Cream | #F5F0E8 | Content sections, cards, page base |
| Text | Charcoal | #1C1C1C | All body copy, never pure black |
| Surface | White | #FFFFFF | Cards, modals, content containers |
| Dark | Near Black | #1A1A2E | App lesson player background |

### Gold Rule
Amber Gold appears on ONE element per screen. If gold is everywhere it means nothing. It exists only to say "press here" or "this is the number that matters."

### Section Rhythm (website)
Dark Teal → Cream → Dark Teal → Cream → Dark Teal.
Never two dark or two cream sections consecutively.

## 5. Brand Voice

### Direct
Short sentences. Active voice. No jargon. No buzzwords.
✓ *"No French teacher? No problem."*
✗ *"Lang Huey provides innovative pedagogical solutions for language acquisition..."*

### Confident
State facts. Never hedge. Never apologise.
✓ *"Your French class runs today."*
✗ *"We hope this might help your school..."*

### Human
The founder's name and story are always in the room.
✓ *"Built by a Nigerian, for Nigerian schools."*
✗ *"Our proprietary algorithm delivers measurable outcomes..."*

### Specific
Numbers over abstractions. Always.
✓ *"JSS1. Term 1. Lesson 1. Press play."*
✗ *"Comprehensive language coverage across multiple levels..."*

## 7. Visual Language

### Design Style
Flat 3D-style illustration. Warm, Nigerian, expressive. No stock photography of foreign children. No cold corporate imagery. Characters feel like they belong in Lagos.

### Layouts
Asymmetric — text left, visual right. Then visual left, text right. Alternating. Never centered blocks that feel like a slide deck.

### Shapes
Rounded everywhere. Pill buttons. Cards with minimum 12px radius. No sharp corners anywhere in the brand.

### Imagery Rules
- Smartboard always shown in a classroom context
- Classroom feels Nigerian — warm tones, recognisable environment
- Teacher figures are non-specialist staff, not language experts
- Students are secondary school age, JSS/SS level
- Never show a "perfect" classroom — show a real one

---

## 8. What Lang Huey Never Does

- Uses more than 3 colours in any single layout
- Compares itself to Duolingo or any foreign app
- Uses stock photos of foreign children in classrooms
- Puts gold on decorative elements — gold is functional only
- Uses buzzwords: AI-powered, revolutionary, world-class, innovative
- Mentions subscription, monthly fee, or recurring cost anywhere
- Makes promises it cannot keep before the product is built
- Pretends to be bigger than it is — honesty is the brand
