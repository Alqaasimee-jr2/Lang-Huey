Solving the Foreign Language Teacher Shortage in Nigerian Secondary Schools

Prepared for: School Owners, Directors, & Boards of Governors | Subject: JSS1–SSS3 French Curriculum Delivery

The Operational Challenge

Qualified French teachers in Nigeria are scarce, expensive, and have high turnover rates. Vacant timetable slots leave students unprepared for mandatory WAEC and BECE examinations, while virtual classes drain school budgets and fail over unstable internet.

The Lang Huey Solution

Lang Huey is an offline language lesson delivery engine installed directly on your Smartboard. Using our proprietary Teacher Cue Bar, any staff member can facilitate complete, structured French lessons without speaking French themselves.

Core Solution Architecture

  • 100% NERDC Curriculum Aligned: Complete coverage of Ministry of Education Term 1, 2, and 3 schemes of work for JSS1 French.
  • 100% Offline Engine: Zero internet dependency. Works reliably every single class period regardless of Wi-Fi status or power outages.
  • Zero-Knowledge Teacher Facilitation: Step-by-step on-screen English prompts guide the teacher (*"Play audio twice. Have Row 1 repeat 'Bonjour'."*).
  • One-Time Ownership License: Hardware-locked single payment model. No monthly subscriptions or recurring fees.

The Lang Huey Institutional Guarantee

Lang Huey guarantees 100% offline operational reliability, exact NERDC curriculum mapping, and zero recurring monthly fees. Your school owns the software license permanently for the bound Smartboard device.
